# Semantic elevation (candidate)

Resina elevation roles are `embedded`, `base`, `raised`, `floating`, `overlay`, and `modal`, ordered from lower to greater visual separation. Elevation expresses a surface's relationship to nearby content. It may influence edge, thickness, highlight, shadow, and background separation; it is not a direct shadow or stacking-index command. `modal` names the strongest separation in this scale and does not itself impose interaction modality.

Every surface form names its elevation explicitly through the [surface form contract](09-geometry.md). The role is renderer-independent and remains meaningful when advanced shadows or transparency are unavailable. Complete numerical realization and component-specific restrictions require calibrated tokens and fallback rules.

An [elevation depth assignment](../schemas/elevation-depth-assignments.schema.json) binds each of the six roles to one nonempty DTCG token path. The bound token MUST be a valid `dimension` with a nonnegative value in logical `px`. Its value contributes to the intended visible side-plane extent; zero requests no elevation depth contribution. The [assignment](../conformance/elevation/depth-assignment-vectors.json) and [resolution](../conformance/elevation/depth-resolution-vectors.json) vectors require all roles, reject invalid bindings, and publish no partial mapping. The role order does not require these extents to increase numerically: edge, highlight, pigment, and other channels may supply separation.

This extent does not choose a side pigment, edge color, light direction, inset treatment, or renderer drawing primitive. Those decisions depend on the material, bound body, and actual surrounding surface. A depth extent alone is not a complete Tier 0 appearance or render-ready Resina IR.
