# Opaque material pigment (candidate, 0.1.0)

An opaque dimensional surface needs body-derived side pigment and highlight pigment. This operation supplies the opaque pigment prerequisite for blueprint §§18, 31, and 43. It consumes a material family, one opaque sRGB body, and complete [pigment profiles](../schemas/opaque-pigment-profiles.schema.json). The caller MUST obtain the family from semantic material resolution and the body from its selected opaque fallback or successful opaque readability result. The operation MUST NOT infer either from a renderer, toolkit, theme name, or component identity.

Every profile supplies `sideShade` and `highlightLift`, finite proportions in `[0, 1)`. A strength of zero preserves the body in that channel. Strength one is excluded because it replaces every body with pure black or pure white and loses its pigment. These are encoded sRGB modulation proportions, not transmission, reflectance, illumination energy, or output alpha. All four family profiles are required and validated, including unselected profiles. Missing fields, unknown fields or families, unsupported versions, and out-of-range values MUST fail without defaulting.

For each encoded sRGB body channel `b`, the resolver MUST calculate `side = b × (1 − sideShade)` and `highlight = highlightLift + b × (1 − highlightLift)`. This is the [source-over compositing rule](https://www.w3.org/TR/2015/CR-compositing-1-20150113/#simplealphacompositing) with black or white at the authored strength over an opaque body. The arithmetic MUST use encoded sRGB components, not linear-light sRGB or a perceptual color space. The output body MUST equal the input body; all three output colors MUST be opaque sRGB. The operation does not sample a backdrop. It MUST reject a translucent body rather than silently discarding alpha or flattening onto an invented background. Frost therefore needs its valid `opaqueDimensional` fallback before this operation is used.

The [reference profiles](../definitions/tier0-pigment.json) use restrained Cast modulation, a brighter Frost highlight, a stronger Elastomer side, and a more pronounced transient Gel highlight. They were calibrated against the authored light and dark theme bodies. They are an explicit reference choice; custom profiles are allowed. Exact black and white bodies can leave one derived channel unchanged at its gamut endpoint. This is a valid mathematical result; edge, geometry, and the other appearance channels remain necessary for identity.

The [request](../schemas/opaque-pigment-request.schema.json) and [result](../schemas/opaque-pigment-result.schema.json) publish version `0.1.0`. The result records the selected family, selected profile, body, side, and highlight so the decision can be inspected. The [profile vectors](../conformance/materials/opaque-pigment-profile-vectors.json) and [operation vectors](../conformance/materials/opaque-pigment-vectors.json) cover every family, custom zero modulation, saturated and gamut endpoint bodies, coefficient boundaries, complete validation, and failure behavior. Numeric results use an absolute tolerance of `1e-12` per normalized channel, with no relative tolerance.

`resina-opaque-pigment <path|->` reads one UTF-8 request up to 1 MiB and emits one JSON result with exit status 0 and no diagnostic. Invalid input exits 1 with a diagnostic and no result; usage errors exit 2. Duplicate JSON members are rejected at every depth. The [external checker](../tools/check_opaque_pigment_backend.py) tests the same source-to-result protocol and deterministic output without importing the Rust implementation.

These colors do not choose edge geometry, light direction, side-plane extent, highlight placement, shape, active-state response, or treatment rendering. A backend MUST NOT place derived pigment behind content without rerunning content contrast for that actual background. Side pigment is distinct from the contrast-guarded outline color. This operation is a composable appearance channel, not complete Resina IR or a claim of complete Tier 0 identity.
